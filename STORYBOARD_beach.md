**Logline:** Clawd wakes up wanting a day at the sea, so Clawd grabs the ukulele, collects three friends across
town, and the four of them walk out of the city, over the fields and the mountain to the beach, play all day, and
watch the sun go down together.

**Cast** (all Clawds, same body, told apart by costume; each friend carries the thing they'll do at the beach):
- **Clawd**: no hat, a honey-coloured ukulele. The lead. Plays the uke at home and at the jam.
- **Spike**: red headband, a volleyball. Bouncy, sporty. Leads the volleyball.
- **Floatie**: swim goggles on the forehead, a pink-and-cream striped float ring round the middle. Dreamy. Leads the swim.
- **Shades**: sunglasses, a wide straw sun hat, a striped parasol. Cool. Leads the sunbathing.

**World:** one bright hand-painted day, from dawn to dusk. Travel always runs left → right.
Colour arc: dawn lilac and peach (home) → fresh morning pastels (city) → green-gold noon (pasture) → cool clear blue
(mountain) → saturated turquoise and warm sand (beach) → gold afternoon (jam) → orange, rose and violet (sunset) →
indigo dusk with the first stars.

**Motifs:**
- **The sun**: rising in Clawd's bedroom window at the start, setting into the sea at the end.
- **The ukulele**: Clawd's first strum wakes the day; Clawd's last strum ends it.
- **Sleep**: Clawd wakes up in the first shot and nods off on a friend's shoulder in the last. The film ends where it
  began, changed: alone in bed at sunrise → together on the sand at sunset.

**Clawd's arc:** sleepy → surprised (the sunbeam) → idea/excited (the beach picture) → happy (strumming) → playful
(the knock) → laugh (the ball on the head) → happy on the road → confused (the sheep) → tired (the climb) → hopeful
(the gull) → determined → starstruck (the sea) → excited (volleyball, swim) → relieved/sleepy (sunbathing) → laugh (the
tan line) → happy singing (jam) → surprised (the sun touches the sea) → love (the sunset) → sleepy (the end).

**No text anywhere.** Singing is wordless: mouth shapes on the beat and painted notes.
