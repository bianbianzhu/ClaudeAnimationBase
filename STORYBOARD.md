# Sunlit Strum: storyboard

Music: `assets/sunlit-strum.mp3` (Suno, instrumental), 123 BPM, first downbeat 0.12 s, 15.6 s.
Beat = 0.488 s, bar = 1.951 s. Bar lines: 0.12 · 2.07 · 4.02 · 5.97 · 7.93 · 9.88 · 11.83 · 13.78.

**Logline:** On a grey morning Clawd wants the sun to come out, so Clawd plays it a song. Every note nudges the
cloud aside, until the sun breaks through, lights Clawd up, and the whole hill blooms along to the tune.

**World:** one grassy hilltop, a sun hidden behind a fat cloud, a violet guitar. Colour arc: grey-blue and muted
(the sun is asleep behind the cloud) → warm, saturated and sunlit. Clawd starts blue-tinted and gloomy and ends gold.

**Motif:** the sun. It's hidden and asleep at the start, then peeks out, breaks through, bops to the beat, and winks
back at the end. The last shot closes on a sunburst-shaped iris.

**Clawd's arc:** sad (looks up at the cloud) → determined (looks at the guitar) → hopeful (watches the note fly) →
surprised → excited → happy, singing → blissful (eyes shut) → surprised → starstruck (the big note) → happy dance →
determined (wind-up) → excited (jump) → proud.

**Singing:** the track is instrumental, so Clawd sings without words. The mouth shapes change on every beat and
painted notes float up. There is no text anywhere.

## Checks

- Every shot has an event: the bonk, the sun breaking out, the blooming and the finale.
- Reads run in sequence: each cause comes before its reaction, and important reads don't overlap.
- There is a transition at every seam: iris in, cut on action, brush wipe, sunburst iris out.
- There is no text anywhere.
- The ending rhymes with the opening: the same hill, grey then sunny, and a hidden sun that ends up winking.
