Where can you be, my love?
I can’t reach you tonight.
Why don’t you answer your phone?
Have you vanished from my life?
Are you trying to run away,
Leaving nothing left to say?
Every silent passing hour
Steals a little of my power.

Pre-Chorus
When I met you, I believed
You were the one meant for me.
Side by side through every day,
You would never walk away.

Chorus
Now you escape…
What have I done wrong?
I need to know
Before you’re really gone.
I fear to stay alone,
Love has always been my way.
I can’t help this aching heart
Since your love went away.

Verse 2
Every memory calls your name,
Nothing feels the same.
Empty rooms and sleepless nights
Hide my tears from morning light.
If I hurt you, let me know,
Don’t disappear without a word.
One last chance is all I pray,
Don’t let love just fade away.

Bridge
If there’s still a spark inside,
Don’t let all our dreams just die.
Take my hand, don’t close the door,
We can find what we had before.

Final Chorus
Now you escape…
What have I done wrong?
I need to know,
I’ve waited for so long.
I fear to stay alone,
Love is still my only way.
My heart will never cease to weep
Until you find your way.

Outro
Where can you be, my love?
I’m still waiting here for you…
